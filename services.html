FAQ

Questions, answered

The things brands usually ask us before getting started. Anything else, just ask on a call.

Ask us directly →
How much does it cost? +
We don't offer fixed packages, so it depends on what your brand needs. After a free strategy call we'll put together a plan and a price tailored to your brand.
Which email platforms do you work with? +
We currently work with Klaviyo. If you're on a different platform, we can talk about whether we can support it, or help you move to Klaviyo.
Do I have to sign up for the long term? +
No. We work on monthly retainers, so you can stop at any time. Some brands just need a one-off flow set-up, others may want us to manage their email long term.
How involved do we need to be? +
As much or as little as you like. If you want to be hands-off, we handle everything from writing and design to set-up; you just sign off on each email before it goes out.
How soon will we see results? +
Most clients see noticeable results within 60 days, with continued growth over time as we keep testing and optimising. We aim to have a new sign-up pop-up and your first core automations live within the first 14 days.
