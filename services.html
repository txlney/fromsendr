FAQ

Questions, answered

The things brands usually ask us before getting started. Anything else, just ask on a call.

Ask us directly →
How much does it cost? +
We don't offer fixed packages, so it depends on what your brand needs. After a free strategy call we'll put together a plan and a price tailored to your brand and needs.
Which email platforms do you work with? +
We are most familiar with and currently work with Klaviyo. If you use a different platform, we can discuss whether we can support it or help you migrate to Klaviyo.
Do I have to sign up for the long term? +
No. We work using monthly retainers, so you can opt to stop at any time. Some brands just need a one-off flow set-up, others may want us to manage their email long term.
How involved do we need to be? +
As much or as little as you like. If you want to be hands-off, we handle everything from writing and design to set-up, you just need to sign off on anything before it goes out.
How soon will we see results? +
Most clients see noticeable results within 60 days, with continued growth over time as we keep testing and optimizing. We aim to design a new site pop-up and the first core automations within the 14 days.
