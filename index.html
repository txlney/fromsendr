The mistakes we see most

Outdated flows

Automations set up once and forgotten, still sending last year's offers to this year's customers.

One-off campaigns

A send here and there, with long silences in between, so your list forgets who you are.

Sending with no plan

Too many random campaigns with no clear strategy, training your customers to tune out.
