How we work

Email marketing, handled end to end

Most brands end up juggling a designer, a copywriter and someone who "does the Klaviyo stuff". Things fall through the gaps between them. We handle every part of your email marketing ourselves, so the creative, the tech and the strategy all pull in the same direction.

01

Copy & design

On-brand copy and mobile-first design, written and built to work together.

A good-looking email still needs a reason to click. We write in your brand's voice and design each layout around the message, so every email is easy to read and easy to act on.

02

Flows & automation

Automated emails that turn sign-ups into customers, around the clock.

Automated emails reach people at the moment they're most likely to buy, around the clock. We plan, build and maintain the flows that turn sign-ups into customers and customers into regulars.

03

Campaign strategy

Campaigns planned around your launches, seasons and stock.

We plan your campaigns around your launches, seasons and stock, so every email has a clear reason to be sent, and your list hears from you often enough without tiring of you.

04

Deliverability

A healthy sender reputation and segments that keep you out of spam.

Even the best email in the world is worthless in a spam folder. We keep your sender reputation healthy, your list clean, and segment your audience so each group only gets the emails they care about.

05

Constant A/B testing

We test subject lines, layouts and send times to find what really works.

Guesses about what will work are often wrong. We test everything, from subject lines and layouts to send times and offers, so decisions are based on what your audience actually does.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The things brands usually ask us before getting started. Anything else, just ask on a call.

Ask us directly →
How much does it cost? +
We don't offer fixed packages, so it depends on what your brand needs. After a free strategy call we'll put together a plan and a price tailored to your brand.
Which email platforms do you work with? +
We currently work with Klaviyo. If you're on a different platform, we can talk about whether we can support it, or help you move to Klaviyo.
Do I have to sign up for the long term? +
No. We work on monthly retainers, so you can stop at any time. Some brands just need a one-off flow set-up, others may want us to manage their email long term.
How involved do we need to be? +
As much or as little as you like. If you want to be hands-off, we handle everything from writing and design to set-up; you just sign off on each email before it goes out.
How soon will we see results? +
Most clients see noticeable results within 60 days, with continued growth over time as we keep testing and optimising. We aim to have a new sign-up pop-up and your first core automations live within the first 14 days.
