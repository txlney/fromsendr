About us

Our story

We noticed an issue in the email marketing space: too many brands needed an email overhaul, but had to weigh up great design and copy vs. great strategy and deliverability. We wanted to create a service that could reliably provide both, integrating into and working alongside your brand to produce effective email flows and campaigns that are on-brand, on-message, and on-time.

Having worked with e-commerce brands for years before turning to email marketing, we understand how successful the email channel can become. There is nothing worse than seeing a brand with a great product and a great story, but no real email strategy to back it up.

That's why we want to help brands like yours, to become clients like ours, and unlock the full potential of your email marketing channels.

How we're different

Many agencies focus on either the creative side of email marketing, or the technical side. We are passionate about both, and work to ensure your marketing is both visually stunning and technically sound. We don't want to treat you like a client, we want to work with your brand and become a valued partner, with the goal of growing your brand as if it were our own.
